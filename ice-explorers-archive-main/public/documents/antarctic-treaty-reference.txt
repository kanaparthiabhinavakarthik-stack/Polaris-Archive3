Title: The Antarctic Treaty — Reference Summary
Year: 1959
Discipline: Polar governance
Access: Public

SUMMARY
The Antarctic Treaty was signed in 1959 and established a framework for peaceful international cooperation in Antarctica. It supports freedom of scientific investigation and cooperation while restricting military activity in Antarctica.

WHY IT MATTERS
The treaty provides an important historical and legal foundation for modern Antarctic research cooperation.
