Title: ICESat-2 and Polar Ice-Surface Measurements
Year: 2018–present
Discipline: Glaciology / Earth observation
Access: Public

SUMMARY
NASA's ICESat-2 mission measures elevation using a photon-counting laser altimeter. Its measurements support research into ice-sheet elevation, glaciers, sea ice and other changes on Earth's surface.

KEY POINTS
• ICESat-2 launched on September 15, 2018.
• Its ATLAS instrument uses six laser beams.
• Measurements can be used to study changes in polar ice.
• NASA provides ICESat-2 data through Earth-science data systems.
