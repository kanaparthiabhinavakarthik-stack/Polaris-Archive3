Title: Antarctic Ozone Research — Reference Record
Discipline: Atmospheric science / Climate

SUMMARY
The Antarctic ozone hole is a recurring seasonal reduction in stratospheric ozone over Antarctica. Scientific monitoring combines satellite observations, atmospheric measurements and long-term records to understand ozone chemistry and recovery.

RESEARCH USE
This record is an introductory reference for polar atmosphere, ozone chemistry, satellite monitoring and climate-related observations.
